Private Label & OEM

End‑to‑end contract manufacturing solutions for global leather glove brands — from concept to production.

As a premier, vertically integrated leather gloves manufacturer, we offer comprehensive Private Label and Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) services tailored to international importers, safety distributors, and premium apparel brands. Operating under strict regulatory compliances and global quality benchmarks, we transform raw, high-grade hides into high-performance hand protection, backed by precision engineering, technical documentation support, and robust scale capacity.

Private Labeling (White Label)

Accelerate your market entry with our catalog of field-proven, market-ready glove designs. Seamlessly integrate your branding into established, high-demand products.

  • Custom Branding: High-definition logo embossing, debossing, heat transfers, and premium woven labels.
  • Packaging & Retail Ready: Custom header cards, barcodes (EAN/UPC), specialized hangtags, and compliant polybags.
  • Low Initial Investment: Lower Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs) utilizing our pre-tested, standardized glove patterns.

OEM Manufacturing

Bring your proprietary designs, material blueprints, and unique technical specifications to life. We offer complete custom tooling and structural engineering from scratch.

  • Bespoke Pattern Development: Custom sizing, ergonomic adjustments, and specialized thread configurations.
  • Advanced Fabric Fusion: Combining premium leather with Kevlar®, Nomex®, high-grip silicones, or impact-resistant TPR shells.
  • Confidentiality Assured: Strict intellectual property protection and exclusive mold/pattern retention.
