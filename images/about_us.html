Profile

Premier Exports proudly presents itself as a leading leather manufacturer and exporter, delivering premium quality leather products and made-ups. Every item is manufactured in our production unit in Lahore with meticulous care and the highest standards of workmanship. Customers can choose from sheep, goat, cow, or buffalo leather in a wide range of colors and sizes from our catalogue — all backed by our commitment to unmatched quality at competitive prices.

The leather industry of Pakistan stands as the second-largest export-oriented sector in manufacturing, and ranks third in its contribution to the National Exchequer. Its core products include leather jackets, gloves, bags, garments, tanned leather, and footwear.

Premier Exports operates a fully in-house production facility supported by a dedicated and competent team that understands both our company philosophy and each customer's individual requirements. Our facility covers every stage of production — leather tanning, processing, bleaching, dyeing, calendering, raising, cutting, stitching, pressing, packing, and quality control.
