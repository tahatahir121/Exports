Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a premier manufacturer and trusted exporter of high-grade industrial safety gloves, athletic handwear, and protective hospital attire—setting industry benchmarks in safety, ergonomic design, and technological sustainability.

Our Mission

To deliver uncompromised safety and comfort to workforce professionals worldwide by combining rigorous quality control, ethical leather sourcing, advanced stitching technology, and custom manufacturing solutions tailored to global client specifications.

Procurement & Sourcing

  • Certified Raw Materials: High‑tensile Kevlar yarns, breathable textiles, barrier fabrics, and impact‑resistant TPR pads are sourced from vetted international and certified domestic vendors.
  • Chemical & Compliance Checks: All textiles and dyes undergo REACH‑standard chemical screening to ensure zero hazardous substances in hospital wear and safety gear.

Leather Selection Standard

  • Premium Hides: Hand‑selection of prime Goat, Cow, and Sheep hides featuring optimal grain density and tensile strength.
  • Grade Grading & Flaw Control: Leather splits are graded for uniform thickness, soft touch, flame retardancy, and water repellency prior to cutting.

Future Expansion Plans

To keep pace with growing global demand and advance manufacturing capabilities, we are executing a strategic growth roadmap:

1. Production Expansion to 20,000 Pairs/Day

Upgrading stitching lines and adding automated leather splitting machines in Lahore to scale output by 66% over the next 18 months.

2. Sustainable & Eco‑Friendly Tanning

Transitioning 40% of leather processing to chrome‑free vegetable tanning to align with EU eco‑directive standards.

3. Advanced Medical Attire Unit

Establishing a dedicated clean‑room facility for fluid‑resistant hospital gowns and sterile surgical garments.

4. R&D in Impact & Anti‑Vibration Handwear

Integrating specialized TPR molding and Kevlar lining technology for heavy‑duty oil and gas work gloves.
