Our Commitment to Excellence

It gives me great pleasure to introduce PREMIER EXPORTS (PRIVATE) LIMITED, an organization committed to offering the most innovative and outstanding quality products with our diversified technology and expertise. Our philosophy is to provide "Customer-centric" products at the least possible prices. This philosophy has been cultivated in our staff since its inception. Our focus is to carve a niche in today's glove manufacturing industry and at the same time provide consistent quality services with the motto "Customer First".

As a professional learning organization, our priority is to respond to our customer's growing demand. To achieve this goal, we have hired professional staff along with seasoned senior management of proven track record. We strictly adhere to our corporate philosophy, to become a leading company of the world. Your continued support in this regard will be highly appreciated. Our production Unit in Lahore has a production capacity of up to 10,000 pairs of gloves in a single day. We are planning to increase it to 15,000 pairs in the future.

We have a clear long-term strategy which is based on our purpose, vision, mission and values. It is this foundation which helps us successfully shape the future of our company. In future, we want to generate continued profitable growth, to become more customer-focused, more innovative, more agile and fully digitized in our internal processes and customer-facing activities. To achieve this ambition, we will focus on our strategic priorities and financial commitments for the coming years. In addition, we aim to promote sustainability in all our business activities, reinforcing our position.
